About GeoQuiz

GeoQuiz is an interactive web-based quiz designed to test your knowledge of geography. It features a clean, responsive design, and dynamically fetches random geography questions from The Trivia API. Whether you are a student, a geography enthusiast, or just looking for a fun way to learn, GeoQuiz is the perfect tool to challenge yourself.
